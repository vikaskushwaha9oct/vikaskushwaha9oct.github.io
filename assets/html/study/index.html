Survey Exploring Code Understandability

* Participation is this study is entirely voluntary. Please take the survey individually -- not in groups.

* You are eligible to participate if you are 18 years of age or older, and have basic programming knowledge or above in either Java or Python (familiarity with classes and inheritance is expected).

* If you choose to participate, you will be required to understand small code snippets and then answer few questions related to understanding them. It should take about 20 minutes of your time. Taking this survey on laptop or desktop is preferred as it involves reading code snippets side by side.

* There are no direct benefits or compensation for participating in this study, but the resulting research might eventually improve the tools you use while programming.

* Any information we collect about you will be anonymised before analyzing to ensure that your identity cannot be linked to the study data we publish. Email address we collect is only to ensure there are no duplicate responses.

* For more queries on this study, please contact: Vikas Kushwaha (vikask23@cse.iitk.ac.in).

* If you have any questions about the rights of research study participants, please contact the Institutional Ethics Committee (iec@iitk.ac.in).

* If you wish, you may leave the survey at any point until you click the "Finish" button. Your responses will be discarded and won't be stored on the server before that point.
